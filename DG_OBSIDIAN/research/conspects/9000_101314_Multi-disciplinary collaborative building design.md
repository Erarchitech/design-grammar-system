[[101314_Multi-disciplinary collaborative building design.pdf]]

- MAS is a powerful tool to facilitate the collaboration between
distributed parties. An autonomous agent has the following characteristics:
(1) it is not controlled or managed by other agents; (2) it can
communicate/interactwith other agents in the systemand also with other
external systems; (3) it can learn fromother agents and its environment;
and (4) it has its own goals and an associated set of motivations. Agents are best suited for applications that are modular, decentralised, rapidly changing, ill-structured, large-scale, complex and multi-disciplinary

- MAS allows individual areas of expertise to be encoded into particular agents
- Some of the other benefits of agent-based systems include: 
	- decentralisation of traditional and inadequate project command and control structures;
	- effective decomposition of large-scale problems; 
	- improved collaborative and concurrent working and easier and cheaper access to specialist information.
- an agent-based system is a loosely coupled network of problem solvers that work together to solve problems that are beyond each individual's capabilities
- Agent technologies are used to:
	- enable incremental and large-scale process and product knowledge acquisition; 
	- facilitate design process knowledge reuse; 
	- and support distributed dynamic process management
- Agents negotiate between themselves to arrive at a consensual design that
satisfies each of them.
- Types of Agents:
	- Domain Agents:
		- Interface/architectural agent (IAA)
		- Specialist agents (SDA (Structural Design Agent), BSA, etc)
			- SDA: This agent has structural design knowledge. It evaluates the proposed structure and can suggest changes.
			- BSA: This agent has the design knowledge of heating, lighting and other building services. It, again, can return suggestions for alterations as above
- In order for the domain agents to communicate effectively, they
need to have a common understanding and shared knowledge of the
concepts associated with the design. In other words, the agents should
have a ==**domain-specific ontology.**== In the case of collaborative building
design, the construction industry already has a vocabulary for
describing the different elements. This is encapsulated in design
==standards such as BS 5950==, and Technical Reports from the
International Standards Organisations—==ISO TR14177==.
