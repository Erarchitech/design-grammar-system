[[9000_103117_Cloud-based Collaboration]]

- This study develops a series of components to implement a previously established Cloud BIM (CBIM) platform that facilitates fileless cloud collaboration across BIM disciplines
- AEC industry is characterized by its ==fragmented nature== in the sense that numerous stakeholders with diverse specialties are involved throughout the lifecycle of a building project.


![[Pasted image 20241219164942.png]]

![[Pasted image 20241219165221.png]]

The method is rather translational than generative as it translates domain-specific BIM model into CBIM Graph and then translates it back to other model as reference. It does not compute semantics autnomously (except topologic relationships) neither assess the design outputs.
==CBIM focuses on geometric elements tranlation and design changes tracking.==
In fact, the workflow described in the paper replicates the traditional linking model approach.
