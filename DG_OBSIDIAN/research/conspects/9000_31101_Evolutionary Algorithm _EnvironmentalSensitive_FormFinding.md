WallaceiX plugin: Wallacei uses the Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm (NSGA-II) as the primary algorithm and the K- means method as a clustering algorithm. Optimum solutions are extracted, the fitness function value is analyzed, and the solutions with similar characteristics are identified using the K-means clustering method.

RESULTS 
With three fitness values per solution, the algorithm's run resulted in 1500 genotypes/phenotypes, or 45,000 values in total. Visually analyzing the building forms of each solution is extremely ineffective and, more significantly, dependent on the user's subjective visual evaluation. Statistical analysis of the generated solutions is crucial to selecting and altering the best solutions. An examination of the evolutionary simulation's overall performance and how well it met the fitness objectives were the primary steps in evaluating the MOO process.
