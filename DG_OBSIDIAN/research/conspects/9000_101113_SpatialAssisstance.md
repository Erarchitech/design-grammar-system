[[101113_MSAS_SpatialAssisstance.pdf]]


Describes methodoology for Extraction of semantic model by robot based on 3d Cloud scanning results, detecting different classes of objects in interior spaces (doors, walls, floors) and conceptualising them according to defined ontology. Extracted semantics and conceptualised data is computed and recorded in MSAS online system. It enables quantitative and qualitative queries of operator as well as spatial assessment according tu rules defined.

Usefull for my research from the point of implementing Semantic Model based on Ontology. The source of data instead of 3dCloud can be presented by other design inputs.
