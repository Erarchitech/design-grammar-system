Computational Architectural Integrated Implementation: A Digital Process from Initial Design to Fabrication.

Nowadays the whole process from initial design to implementation, and to mainte
nance are information based and digitized. This inspired the authors to introduce science and engineering knowledge such as computer science (CS) theories, management science and information technologies into the field of architectural design.

Current requirements of architectural designs include BIM, non-standard design expressions, and performance optimized. Typical architectural designs
rely on the processing of a wide range of information. Theoretically, all these data
form the overall ==information system of the building==.

Information systems manage design processes and are based on these main functions: information collection, processing, and transfer.

Besides conventional architectural challenges, competitive requirements of localization, performance and sustainability are increasing. This requires that the same building data has to be analyzed to addressn different aspects.

In summary, design and fabrication processes can
be defined as a ==process of information evolution.==

The higher the degree of automation, the more critical is the integrity of the information used to control the machines.
