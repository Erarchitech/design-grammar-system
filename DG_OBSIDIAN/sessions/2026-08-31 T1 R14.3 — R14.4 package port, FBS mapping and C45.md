## What happened

### 1. S2 FBS↔Design State mapping → § 3.3

R14.3's § 3.3 listed the three state kinds without saying which FBS role each held. Ported from
R14.4, in the tone pass's register (R14.4's wording predates it and carried a cleft plus a
`rather than` foil in a paragraph already holding four).

- ObjState → **Structure**, PropState → **Function**; ParamState → **Behaviour** was already there.
- Prerequisite found: the tone pass had dropped the word `moment`, leaving
  `at a given (Figure 5)` — so *"at that moment"* in the new text had no antecedent. Author fixed it.

### 2. C46 — resolved by the author

`"What 3 exclusions?"` — § 2.2 now names them: generated vocabulary, IFC alignment, movement between
Design States.

### 3. C45 — the interesting part

`"Why not presented? It should be presented now, check other sections."` Checking the other
sections found three inconsistent claims about the same thing, with § 2.2's `is not represented` the
only false one — and the S2 port had just sharpened the contradiction.

R14.4 answers this with a derivation rule in § 6.1: a typed relation between **successive** Design
States, where a differing `PropState` means the Function was recast (reformulation). Two author
corrections killed it:

1. **T1 fixes a pre-implementation project stage** — the paper is the foundation for later schema
   changes, so it need not match the implemented ontology. This *removed* an objection raised
   earlier in the session.
2. **A Design State trail is not necessarily forward or backward** — states may be alternatives in
   one design space, belong to different design spaces (facade vs plan layout of one building), or
   divide by typology within one space (hexagonal glazed vs timber structural facade sets).

Correction 2 defeats the rule twice over: there is no single successor, and — checked against the
framework literature — **reformulation redefines a state space rather than linking two states**
(`S→S'`, `S→Be'`, `S→F'`). A pairwise diff therefore cannot tell *an alternative admitted by the
current Function space* from *the Function space was recast*. The defect is in the rule, not just in
the word *successive*.

### 4. The series boundary

[[../dissemination/Series coherence map|Series coherence map]] assigns `DesignStateSnapshot` and
`REINSTATE` to **T3** — whose case study is already *3 massing alternatives, 9 runs* with
**REINSTATE for non-linear navigation** — and `DesignSpaceGraph`/`MetricSpec` to **T4**. T1's row for
Design State is a dash. So the design-space structure is T4's contribution and T1 must not
formalise it.

§ 6.1 therefore states the **precondition** — recording which design space and partition a state
belongs to — instead of a derivation that cannot work. Author chose no companion citation, so the
handoff stays an open direction.
