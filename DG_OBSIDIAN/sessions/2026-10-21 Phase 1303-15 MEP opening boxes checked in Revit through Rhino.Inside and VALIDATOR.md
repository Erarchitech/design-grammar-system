## Decisions Made

- Evaluation runs through Rhino.Inside.Revit → Grasshopper VALIDATOR. DeGram starts the check; the user never opens `_ScriptEditor`.
- Scripts are generated for any new rule, stored, and re-run for a new Design State or a rule update.
- DG creates every class a rule involves at rule creation (e.g. Opening + Column).
- Rule deletion also removes the orphaned dependent nodes.
- Duplicate classes are checked during ingest, with operator confirmation; the Reasoner consistency check lists them too.
- D-54: a generated calculator joins the shared catalogue only after operator confirmation ([[knowledge/decisions/Generated property calculators are promoted to the catalogue after operator confirmation|decision]]).
- The Model Viewer shows, per rule, only that rule's classes, in their real geometry.
- The DG mapping outranks the Revit category for calculators (this session).
