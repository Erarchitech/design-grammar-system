<objective>
The C# half of D-61 (requirement DGOPN-06): a rule that names several object classes and the relations between them must validate exactly like its one-class form. Participants other than the checked object are asserted by the rule, excluded from binding, and disclosed in the result message; genuine multi-object joins keep today's typed `unsupported` refusal.

Purpose: plan 1303-20 lets the LLM write such rules. Without this plan every one of them would turn from checkable into `unsupported` (the evaluator refuses `ObjectPropertyAtom`) or `unknown` (binding rows carry one object per ObjState).
