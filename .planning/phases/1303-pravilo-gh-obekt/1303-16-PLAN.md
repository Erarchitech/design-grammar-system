Wave-0 guards and the store half of D-54 (requirement DGOPN-05 per D-55; decisions D-54, D-56, D-58, D-59, D-60, with D-57 enforced by the routes of plan 1303-17). This plan builds everything the later plans call, and pins it with tests that run without Neo4j:

- a save that never undoes a review decision;
- a blocked-hash read that remembers every rejected source, also on active rows;
- the company-scoped `CatalogueCalculator` node with locked confirm, reject and retire transactions;
- the graph-route guards that keep the new node out of project edits and claim-untagged;
- the REPRODUCIBILITY registration that turns the drift test green, and the DATABASE / LPG-OWL-MAPPING rows.

Purpose: a calculator the LLM wrote for one project may later run in other users' Revit sessions. Its storage must make a confirmation exact, a rejection durable and a retirement final before any route or UI exposes them.

**Retire rights and confirming again (D-59, owner decision 2026-10-21):** an owner of any project in the company may retire a catalogue entry. The store lets a retire request of any project of the entry's company retire the entry, and the cascade reaches the copies in every other project of that company. Plan 1303-17 restricts the route to the owner role of the requesting project. Retire deletes nothing and keeps history (`historyJson`). A retired calculator may be confirmed again later: a confirm of the same sha256, through the same gates, makes the entry and its retired copies active again. A retired hash is therefore paused, not rejected.

**Who confirms a retired calculator again (D-60, owner decision 2026-10-21):** only an owner. The store's `confirm` takes `allow_reconfirm` (default False) and refuses a retired copy without it (`CATALOGUE_RECONFIRM_OWNER_REQUIRED`, 403), inside the same locked transaction that reads the row. Plan 1303-17 sets the flag from the owner role of the requesting project. A first confirm of a pending calculator ignores the flag and stays at editor. A retired copy is also never rejected per project, with or without regenerate (`CALCULATOR_IN_CATALOGUE`), so no path turns a paused hash into a rejected one.
