<objective>
Resolution, vocabulary and routes for D-54 (requirement DGOPN-05 per D-55; decisions D-54, D-56, D-57, D-58, D-59, D-60), built on the store of plan 1303-16:
- `property_scripts.resolve` binds file catalogue, then company catalogue, then project-stored, then LLM, with integrity checks and the rejected-hash guard;
- the ingest vocabulary grows from confirmed company entries only, through a deep copy;
- a pure candidate read model shows source, sample values and verdicts from a published run;
- four routes (candidates, confirm, reject, retire) sit behind deny-by-default policy rows that match the machine-checked SECURITY-BOUNDARY block.

Purpose: a calculator confirmed in one project is reused by every later rule and project of the same company without the LLM. The DeGram agent can list but never decide (D-57), and a rejected calculator is never revived.

**Retire rights and confirming again (D-59, owner decision 2026-10-21):** an owner of any project in the company may retire a catalogue entry. The retire route admits the owner role of the requesting project, and the store cascades to copies in every project of that company, deleting nothing and keeping history. A retired calculator may be confirmed again later: the candidates route lists the project's retired copies, and the confirm route (the same gates as a first confirm) makes the entry and its copies active again.

**Only an owner confirms a retired calculator again (D-60, owner decision 2026-10-21).** The confirm policy row stays editor-level (D-57 unchanged), since a first confirm of a pending calculator stays at editor. The confirm handler passes `allow_reconfirm = auth.effective_role(principal, project) == "owner"` to the store, which refuses a retired copy without it inside its locked transaction (`CATALOGUE_RECONFIRM_OWNER_REQUIRED`, 403). A retired copy is never offered for regeneration: the candidates route lists it once, as a retired candidate, never in `blocked[]`, and the store refuses a reject of a retired copy with or without regenerate (`CALCULATOR_IN_CATALOGUE`).

The Web UI review card, the rollout and the live end-to-end run are plan 1303-18. All work stays in the worktree branch (D-01).
</objective>
