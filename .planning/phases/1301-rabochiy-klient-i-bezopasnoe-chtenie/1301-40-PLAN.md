- Store `connector-access.json` with `grants` `{access_id, username, project, connector_id, issued_by, created_at, revoked, [revoked_at, revoked_by]}` and `requests` `{request_id, username, project, connector_id, note ≤200, created_at, status pending|closed, [closed_at, closed_by_access]}`.

- `GET /connectors?project=P`: when the caller is a member of P (or admin), each non-DeGram connector carries `access`: `available` | `requestable` | `pending`; without `project` the payload is unchanged.
- Membership removal cascades connector access (next to the DeGram cascade at app.py :4871).
