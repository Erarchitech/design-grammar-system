The final report must contain artifact/source hashes, exact commands and outputs, evaluator and subject scope, contract/schema/provenance versions, status and evidence disposition, missing measurements, blockers, and an explicit distinction between automated, live-environment, and human-authorized checks.

## Release criteria

The milestone is release-ready only when:

1. all 37 v11.0 requirements have recorded evidence;
2. every applicable cross-milestone gate has a named record, while future not-yet-activated gates are explicitly marked pending rather than treated as failures;
3. no critical contradiction is unclassified;
4. missing evidence cannot be interpreted as an implicit pass or invalidity;
5. no unsupported Topologic, IFC/bSDD, SHACL, HermiT, interoperability, or determinism claim remains;
6. fresh-checkout checks reproduce the declared contract;
7. principal manuscript and repository manifest agree;
8. remaining human/live blockers are explicit and accepted or remain `INCOMPLETE`;
9. a release-time guard prevents reintroduction of classified V7 drift or broad unsupported claims.
