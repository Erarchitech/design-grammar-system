### D-1104 — Contradiction-first execution

The paper-side R15.2/R15.4 alignment architecture and the committed V8 deposit contain unresolved differences, especially around the status of the Topologic module, the BOT/Topologic module naming, and the absence of a dedicated IFC/bSDD classification module. v11.0 must resolve these as an explicit decision before treating the bundle as publication-ready. ### D-1105 — Compatibility over blind replacement

V7 references may remain when they document historical lineage, compatibility paths, or archived evidence. Current-runtime and publication-facing references must be classified and migrated deliberately. No repository-wide blind V7-to-V8 replacement is allowed.

### D-1106 — Evidence and status boundaries

A successful result is scoped to its evaluator and subject evidence. The common evidence statuses are:

- `VALID`, `INVALID`, `INCOMPLETE`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `SKIPPED_WITH_REASON`, `NOT_CHECKED`;
- operational states such as `UNAVAILABLE`, `TIMEOUT`, `CANCELLED`, `REQUEST_INVALID`, `EXECUTION_ERROR`, `PORT_OCCUPIED`, `PROJECT_UNRESOLVED`, `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, and `PUBLISH_REJECTED`.

Transport failure, missing evidence, stale snapshots, truncation, and unsupported semantics must not become `INVALID` or silently become `VALID`. Legacy Boolean fields remain compatibility fields and are not retroactively reinterpreted.

### D-1107 — Determinism is scoped

Deterministic claims apply only to fixed inputs, fixed snapshots, fixed schema/contract versions, fixed algorithms, and fixed execution configuration. They do not apply to LLM responses, provider fallback, live canvas state, timestamps, live services, or the whole LLM pipeline.

### D-1108 — Validation ownership

The contract distinguishes:

- OWL/TBox consistency over the selected static TBox and overlays;
- runtime ABox projection;
- Neo4j LPG operations and Computgraph structural checks;
- RDF projection used by reasoner/SHACL profiles;
- SHACL structural/data-integrity validation;
- SWRL/DG design-compliance validation;
- future graph-native structure rules;
- LLM and component-KB consumers.

No later phase may merge these into one universal compliance verdict.

### D-1109 — Early gates

Before v11.0 executes, the following early changes are required:

1. Phase 40 receives a narrow V8 publication-contract preflight and evidence-language gate.
2. Standalone GSD control-plane reconciliation executes before v12.0 planning/execution.
3. v9.1 activation requires the shared-surface compatibility preflight **and v12.0 Phases 1200–1203** (canonical status/evidence, parser boundary, replay/per-object verdicts, identity/bridge decision).
4. v10.0 activation, and especially Phase 47, requires the semantic-boundary, fail-closed, rule-partition, and reproducibility preflight **plus v12.0 Phases 1200–1204**.
5. v12.0 Phase 1205 is a release gate for external multi-user evaluation, not a prerequisite for local feature planning.

These are gates and wording corrections, not absorption of the full v11.0 migration into earlier milestones.

1. **Should v11.0 be a ninth-plus milestone?** No. The scope fits nine coherent work packages, so v11.1 is not planned.
2. **Should v11.0 activate now?** No. It remains isolated while v9.0 is active.
3. **Should V8 be copied blindly?** No. The publication bundle must be compared and its Topologic/BOT/IFC boundary reconciled first.
4. **Should Phase 40 own the full migration?** No. It owns only the narrow closeout preflight; v11.0 owns the full migration.
5. **Should v9.1/v10.0 be renumbered?** No. v9.1 remains `910–917`; v10.0 remains `41–49`; v11.0 uses `1101–1109`.
6. **Is graphify evidence of all dirty-tree changes?** No. Graphify output must record its source commit and extraction scope, and must not be treated as evidence for uncommitted files it did not ingest.
