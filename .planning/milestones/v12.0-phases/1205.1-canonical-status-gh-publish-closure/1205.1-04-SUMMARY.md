- `ValidationPublishRuleResult.Warnings: List<string>`, `Detail: string?`; `ValidationPublishEntity.CanonicalWarnings: ordinal Dictionary<string,List<string>>`, `CanonicalDetails: ordinal Dictionary<string,string>`.
