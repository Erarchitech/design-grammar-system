# Requirements: Design Grammar System v14.0 — Normative Sources and Model Binding

**Defined:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Future (isolated; owner accepted a separate milestone, not activated)
**Core value:** An architect can connect a permitted building-code source, review clause-grounded rule and model-binding proposals against an approved standard-linked Ontograph vocabulary, accept selected changes, and trace a measured outcome back to the exact source and approvals. Automation proposes; authorized people approve meaning, publication and project activation.

> **Isolation:** These are future v14 requirements, not the active `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`. Phases 1400–1408 are reserved in [the future roadmap](v14.0-ROADMAP.md). v13 remains active; no `.planning/phases/`, `.planning/STATE.md`, `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` or PROJECT.md Current Milestone changes are implied. Owner approval of the separate milestone is not approval to execute, connect external accounts, send licensed text to a model, deploy or publish.

**Design basis:** [Building Codes integration concept](../../docs/reviews/building-code-validation-integration-analysis/Design%20Grammars%20%C3%97%20Building%20Codes%20%E2%80%94%20%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%86%D0%B5%D0%BF%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F%20%D0%B8%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%B8.md), §§4–9.1. The concept is a proposal; the requirements and phase gates below are the planning baseline, not implementation evidence.

## v14.0 requirements

### Contracts and authorized scope — NCON (1400)

- [ ] **NCON-01**: A versioned normative profile names company/library/jurisdiction/document/edition/clause identity, source authority, applicability, license/data-transfer rules and actor permissions; two companies with the same jurisdiction never share private source text or decision memory. A denied or ambiguous scope fails closed.
- [ ] **NCON-02**: The new normative profile resides in SpecGraph; Ontograph remains the company's vocabulary, Metagraph the project's rules, Computgraph the model/binding layer and ValidGraph the state/outcome layer. A schema/API/status change has an owner and a propagation checklist against the existing mapping, SWRL subset, evidence, reproducibility, partition and security contracts before implementation.
- [ ] **NCON-03**: The v13 consumption ledger identifies exact DGKN-01…06 revision/release/steward/CAS capabilities, DGBN-01…03 GH identity/binding, DGST evidence and DGHN Revit read capabilities actually accepted for reuse; missing or incompatible capabilities block only their dependent v14 slice, rather than being marked complete by reference. The rule-ingest F6/F13 collision/merge risk is assigned an owner and closed before bulk ingest.

### Standard-linked company vocabulary — NVOC (1403)

- [ ] **NVOC-01**: A candidate catalog records a published standard concept URI, exact source vocabulary/version or retrieval date, definition, language, class/property kind, value type/unit where relevant, provenance and individually checked use rights. It references external vocabulary without importing whole IFC/ifcOWL or silently creating company classes.
- [ ] **NVOC-02**: A pinned company release supports type-aware retrieval of existing Ontograph IDs first, then permitted external candidates; proposals distinguish exact/narrower/broader/related/rejected without treating lexical or IFC-export similarity as regulatory equivalence. A new internal term requires steward approval, and duplicate/near-duplicate candidates are exposed for review.
- [ ] **NVOC-03**: Accepted, rejected and ambiguous term-to-concept decisions have explicit company/project and contextual scope, source/release/version, reviewer and evidence; only a still-compatible accepted decision can be reused automatically, while a project decision cannot promote itself to company authority and a scoped rejection cannot ban a concept globally.

### Source connection, enumeration and change detection — NSRC (1401)

- [ ] **NSRC-01**: An authorized operator can ingest a permitted file as a content-addressed fetched snapshot, compare a same-name re-upload by hash and document designation, and distinguish unchanged, changed and different-document cases without overwriting prior snapshots.
- [ ] **NSRC-02**: An authorized operator can connect an allowlisted HTTPS document/catalog URL and a read-only Google Drive folder; an explicit check discovers changes and reports inaccessible/deleted sources as unavailable, not repealed. SSRF, DNS rebinding/redirect, size/time, parser isolation, OAuth scope, encrypted token storage and secret-free logs are verified on negative fixtures before live connection.
- [ ] **NSRC-03**: A deterministic enumerator yields stable clause/table identifiers and hashes, cross-references, edition metadata and a total clause count; a hand-checked corpus tests missed/renumbered/changed clauses and a parser-collapse guard blocks mass retirement on suspicious disappearance. Licensed text is retained or exported only as permitted by source policy.

### Grounded extraction and proposal — NEXR (1402)

- [ ] **NEXR-01**: For each changed clause the pipeline proposes typed requirement/applicability/exception/table data with an exact clause quotation or permitted source coordinates/hash; it verifies that the cited span belongs to the pinned fetched edition and represents the proposed threshold and condition. Unchanged clauses retain previously approved extraction unless a referenced dependency changes.
- [ ] **NEXR-02**: Every proposal distinguishes direct parameter, context/table threshold, measurable geometry/topology, unsupported relational logic and qualitative/manual requirement; ambiguous or unextractable text abstains with a reason, never invents a clause, standard ID or numeric value.
- [ ] **NEXR-03**: A versioned expert gold set contains positive, near-negative and ambiguous cases across permitted clauses, documents and models, with document/model-separated evaluation and a reviewed failure list; candidate recall, duplicate rate, citation correctness and abstention quality are reported before any confidence or automatic-reuse threshold is set.

### Rule compilation and project applicability — NRUL (1404)

- [ ] **NRUL-01**: A deterministic compiler accepts validated typed proposals, pins source clause/revision and selected Ontograph IDs, and emits only the supported violation-pattern SWRL subset; unsupported constructs return the existing appropriate canonical non-verdict path, not a fabricated passing rule. LLM-produced Cypher is not the writer on this route.
- [ ] **NRUL-02**: An authorized steward can review the clause, proposed requirement, selected concept, atom/threshold and measurement basis before accepting a jurisdiction-library rule; project activation separately pins an accepted library revision, jurisdiction/edition, table or national parameter and applicability. Neither library acceptance nor an external source update silently changes active project rules.
- [ ] **NRUL-03**: A project can distinguish inapplicable scope, empty expected population, missing measurement and unsupported evaluation with evidence compatible with the canonical status contract; a historical run retains its original pinned rule/source/binding interpretation after an update.

### Measured Revit/GH binding — NMOD (1405)

- [ ] **NMOD-01**: Three links are proposed and approved independently: norm term → Ontograph concept, Ontograph class → platform object selector, normative property → exact native parameter or versioned tested measurement. Revit category→IFC export data and GH recognition are candidate signals, never proof of compliance meaning.
- [ ] **NMOD-02**: A scoped BindingProfile records approved Revit family/type/instance or GH definition/output identity, unit conversion, measurement/exception semantics, approver and probe evidence; a read-only probe reports selected, excluded, unclassified, missing and stale instances. Ambiguous identity or absent values cannot yield a pass.
- [ ] **NMOD-03**: A deterministic run extracts approved inputs to the existing Design State/evaluator and reports measured value, unit, method/revision, source/rule/binding versions and population coverage without a per-object LLM call. GH final outputs and object/parameter identity are traced end-to-end; M1/M3/M8 are the first bounded slice, with M2/M4–M7 added only after their independent measurement fixtures and native-adapter gates.

### Review, acceptance and history — NCHG (1406)

- [ ] **NCHG-01**: A change screen groups clause-level before/after text or permitted coordinates, requirement, rule, threshold and dependent projects/bindings/states/runs; an authorized reviewer can accept, reject with reason, defer, accept selected or accept all *eligible* items. A rejected unchanged source hash is not redisplayed as a new change without an explicit review action.
- [ ] **NCHG-02**: An immutable fetched source snapshot is separate from a clause-level accepted-source manifest. One compare-and-swap Neo4j transaction commits only compatible selected library clause/requirement/rule revisions with a single server-created acceptanceId/acceptedAt; a conflicting revision writes nothing. Content-addressed blob/CDE storage is staged and verified separately, not claimed to be part of that transaction.
- [ ] **NCHG-03**: Impact records and candidate project revisions link to library acceptance; an authorized project editor reviews and activates each project revision with its own acceptance ID/time. Incompatible deferred clauses block dependent new rules. Both the old and newly fetched editions, partial acceptance count and immutable historical run verdict remain reconstructible.

### Efficient LLM routing and optional Jev — NAI (1407)

- [ ] **NAI-01**: Deterministic IDs/numbers/units, scoped decision memory, kind/unit filtering and retrieval of bounded candidate cards precede any LLM call; a local rule-based router chooses reuse, small model, strong model, request-fact or human. A validated structured model response may select only authorized IDs or abstain/propose a steward-reviewed new term, with bounded retry and no LLM during model evaluation.
- [ ] **NAI-02**: Cache keys and invalidation cover source span/hash and referenced clauses, company/project scope, vocabulary/alignment/binding versions, prompt/schema/model policy and router version; tokens, cost, latency, candidate recall, false bindings, abstentions and operator effort are measured per *accepted correct mapping*, without recording prohibited text or secrets.
- [ ] **NAI-03**: Jev or an equivalent policy adapter is optional and receives only explicitly permitted compact features on unresolved cases. Before enablement, an offline same-corpus A/B/C comparison (always-strong, rule-based+retrieval, rule-based+retrieval+adapter) verifies transfer rights, contract, non-degraded critical-error/coverage bounds and net cost/latency including routing. If unverified or worse than B, B remains the supported path; the adapter has no authority to create concepts or approve writes.

### Pilot acceptance — NUAT (1408)

- [ ] **NUAT-01**: Independent reviewers run the file, website and Drive paths on permitted documents representing at least two distinct jurisdiction profiles and at least one approved Revit and one GH fixture; evidence counts *all* enumerated clauses, eligible/excluded rules, mappings, measured objects, missing data, false passes/fails and manual decisions. Choice of the second jurisdiction and rights to its documents are explicit entry gates, not presumed.
- [ ] **NUAT-02**: A reproducible change/rollback trial includes partial acceptance, same-name/different-document upload, source outage, concurrent steward conflict, project-by-project activation and old-run reconstruction; expected/actual snapshots, audit/read-back and negative privacy/tenant tests are attached. An unavailable measurement or unsupported construct does not become PASS.
- [ ] **NUAT-03**: The release decision uses predeclared quality, traceability, security, recovery and cost gates against raw evidence; unsupported scope and residual v13 debt are disclosed. Conceptual plans, mock diagrams, tool success and unrun tests are not completion evidence.

## Explicit boundaries and open design decisions

- No sixth NormGraph, no imported full IFC ontology, no universal all-jurisdiction parser, no automatic claim that IFC identity proves a building-code term, no model-generated compliance verdict, and no mandatory fine-tuning or Jev dependency. A qualitative rule can remain a review checklist.
- No new status name, SWRL construct, security principal, published route or graph relationship is approved just by this plan: consult and propagate the corresponding normative contracts before implementation. The exact identity/ACL schema, source licensing, steward vs BIM-manager approval, second pilot jurisdiction and Jev contract/transfer rights remain design gates in 1400 or the owning phase.
- v13 DGKN/DGBN/DGST/DGHN/DGRL and WEBUI/HOST remain with their assigned owners. This milestone owns *additional normative behavior*, not a second delivery of their generic capabilities; missing prerequisites are neither autoaccepted nor silently reimplemented.
